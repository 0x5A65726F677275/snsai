# 90-DAY GROWTH STRATEGY → 10M FOLLOWERS

**Account:** art of vector (@art_of_vector27)  
**Start date:** August 14, 2026  
**Current stage:** Pre-launch (0 posts, 0 baseline)

---

## Phase 1 — Discovery (Days 1–30)

**Goal:** Reach strangers. Establish 1:1 video presence. Find winning hooks.

| Element | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Content** | 70% 1:1 square video, 20% 1:1 carousels, 10% stories |
| **Frequency** | 1 post/day weekdays (1:1 video), 1 carousel/weekend |
| **Series launch** | Daily Trend Report (daily), Creator Confessions (2x/week) |
| **Experiments** | Face vs no-face, confession vs educational, trend jack vs evergreen |
| **Metrics focus** | Non-follower reach %, avg watch time, follows per 1K views |
| **Expected learning** | Which hook pattern converts strangers best; optimal Reel length |

**Success criteria (Day 30):** 500+ followers, at least 1 Reel with 10K+ views, follows/1K views > 2.0 on best post

---

## Phase 2 — Recognition (Days 31–60)

**Goal:** Repeatable formats strangers recognize instantly.

| Element | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Content** | Double down on top 2 performing series. Introduce Operator Stack weekly |
| **Frequency** | 5–6 posts/week, maintain daily trend report |
| **Series** | Launch "Trend or Skip" and "From 0 to 10M — Day X" |
| **Experiments** | Collaboration Reels, duet/stitch trend adaptations |
| **Metrics focus** | Returning viewers, save rate, share rate |
| **Expected learning** | Series retention; which pillar drives most follows |

**Success criteria (Day 60):** 2,500+ followers, recognizable cover style, 3+ posts with save rate > 3%

---

## Phase 3 — Community (Days 61–75)

**Goal:** Turn viewers into returning followers who engage daily.

| Element | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Content** | More personality pillar, community Q&A, comment-driven episodes |
| **Frequency** | Maintain cadence + 3–5 story slides/day |
| **Series** | "Things nobody tells you about…" weekly |
| **Experiments** | Live Q&A, poll-driven content decisions |
| **Metrics focus** | Returning viewers, comment rate, DM volume |
| **Expected learning** | What makes people come back vs one-time follow |

**Success criteria (Day 75):** 5,000+ followers, comment rate rising week-over-week

---

## Phase 4 — Scale (Days 76–90)

**Goal:** Double down on winners. Cut losers. Prepare paid amplification.

| Element | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Content** | 80% proven formats with variation, 20% experiments |
| **Frequency** | Scale to 7 posts/week (1:1 video) if metrics hold |
| **Series** | Batch-produce Operator Stack episodes |
| **Experiments** | Paid boost on top 2 organic winners only |
| **Metrics focus** | View-to-follow at scale, cost per follow (if boosting) |
| **Expected learning** | Whether organic winners translate to paid; ceiling on niche reach |

**Success criteria (Day 90):** 10,000+ followers, documented viral formula with 3+ confirmed winners

---

## Long-Term Path to 10M

| Milestone | Followers | Strategy shift |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| Seed | 0 → 10K | Discovery + format testing |
| Traction | 10K → 100K | Series recognition + collaborations |
| Breakout | 100K → 1M | Cross-platform repurpose + brand partnerships |
| Scale | 1M → 10M | Media brand expansion, team, multi-format |

**Critical rule:** At every stage, optimize follows per 1K views — not vanity metrics.
